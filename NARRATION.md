# What If You Woke Up in the Stone Age? — narration script

Storyboard timings: 0–20 · 20–45 · 45–75 · 75–110 · 110–150 · 150–185 · 185–220 s (3:40).
Lines from the brief are **verbatim**. Lines marked ➕ are short additions for pacing and humour (cut them if you prefer the brief only).
`[pause]` = a beat of silence the picture uses (the comedic pause after "supermarket" is the long one).

## 1 · You Wake Up in the Wrong World (0:00)
Imagine waking up one morning and discovering that your entire world has disappeared. No houses. No phones. No electricity. [pause] Just trees, wild animals, and a world where surviving until tomorrow is your biggest achievement.
*On screen:* **WHAT IF YOU WOKE UP IN THE STONE AGE?**

## 2 · Something Is Very Wrong (0:20)
You are somewhere in prehistoric Europe, around 30,000 years ago. Modern civilization is thousands of years in the future. And unfortunately, nobody here has invented a supermarket. **[long comedic pause]**
➕ On the plus side: no traffic. On the minus side: no roads.

## 3 · Meeting the Locals (0:45)
Eventually, you meet some of the locals. [pause] They have skills you don't. They know the landscape, recognize animal tracks, and understand how to make useful tools. [pause] You, meanwhile, have absolutely no idea how to ask where the nearest restaurant is. [pause]
➕ Saying it louder does not help.

## 4 · The Problem With Food (1:15)
Then your stomach starts complaining. [pause] You need food, but there are no shops, no packaged snacks, and no delivery apps. [pause] Finding food requires knowledge, cooperation, and skills you have never needed before. [pause]
➕ Archaeologists find evidence for hunted animals and gathered plants. What they have never found is a fridge.

## 5 · Nature Does Not Care About You (1:50)
And then you notice something watching you from the trees. [long pause] Suddenly, you understand why knowing your environment matters. [pause] Out here, confidence is not a survival skill. Knowing when to back away is. [pause]
➕ Wolves were part of this landscape. Wild, wary, and not interested in being your friend.

## 6 · Learning to Survive (2:30)
Slowly, you begin to understand something. [pause] Survival is not about being the strongest person in the forest. It is about learning, adapting, sharing knowledge, and cooperating with people who understand the world around them. [pause]
➕ Make fire. Shape stone. Build shelter. Share the work. [pause] We can only reconstruct how, from the tools and hearths they left behind.

## 7 · Nightfall (3:05)
By nightfall, you have made it through your first day. You have learned more about survival than you expected. [pause]
➕ Somewhere in the dark, someone is playing a bone flute. Instruments like that are known from Europe at least 35,000 years ago. [pause]
But tomorrow brings another challenge. [pause] And the Stone Age has no emergency hotline.
*On screen:* **COULD YOU SURVIVE YOUR FIRST DAY?** → fade to black.
