# Packaging — Thumbnails, Titles, SEO

## Three title options
1. **How Nokia Lost the Smartphone War** — direct, searchable, matches the episode brand.
2. **Nokia Owned 40% of the World's Phones. Then Everything Changed.** — leads with the verified peak figure (≈40%, Q4 2007 Nokia estimate — F12). Safe because the script states the tracker.
3. **The Real Reason Nokia Fell: Inside the Fear That Sank a Phone Giant** — leans on the Vuori & Huy research (F20, F21). Use only if you're comfortable that "the real reason" is hedged in the video itself as one scholarly reading; if not, swap "The Real Reason" for "A Hidden Reason".

*Avoid:* "the one mistake that killed Nokia" — the script argues there wasn't one.

## Three thumbnail concepts
Design rules: black/gold/white/dark red; max 4 words; one focal object; legible at phone size; no logos; real device photos only.

1. **"THE FALL OF A GIANT"**
   Left: real photo of a **Nokia 3310** (CC BY-SA 4.0, `File:Nokia 3310 grey front.jpg`), gold rim light. Right: background T-A (a cracked silhouette) in dark red. Text: `THE FALL OF A GIANT`. (Avoid a "→ 0%" graphic: Nokia's phone share never literally hit zero in the data used.)
2. **"40% … THEN WHAT?"**
   A huge gold "40%" on black, with the **Nokia 1100 photo** (CC BY-SA 3.0) shrinking beside a dark-red falling arrow. The number is Nokia's own Q4 2007 estimate; add a tiny "Q4 2007" under it.
3. **"THE BURNING PLATFORM"**
   Background G-07 (burning platform at sea), with a **Lumia 800 photo** (CC BY 4.0) small in the foreground, lit by the glow. Text: `THE BURNING PLATFORM`. Ties to the memo story the video covers.

*A/B test suggestion:* concepts 2 and 3. Concept 1 is the safest brand fit.
