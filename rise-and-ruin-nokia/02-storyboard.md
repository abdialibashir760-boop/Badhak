# Shot-by-Shot Storyboard

Times follow `01-script.md` (150 wpm). **Re-time to your audio** — see `05-edit-timeline.md`.

**Asset codes**
- **[C]** Real photograph (Wikimedia Commons / Wikipedia). Used for every named device, person and building. Credit each one — see `assets-manifest/` and the credits block in the video description.
- **[P]** Pixabay stock footage. Candidates are in `assets-manifest/pixabay-candidates.json`. **Preview before using** — search hits are sorted by relevance, not quality (one factory query returned a Halloween monster clip, now excluded). Every [P] shot is tied to a line of narration.
- **[G]** Generated image, for atmosphere or symbolism only (prompts in `03-image-prompts.md`). **Never used to depict a specific phone, person or logo.**
- **[GFX]** Motion graphic (specs in `04-charts-and-timeline.md`).
- **[TXT]** Title/lower-third card.

**Rules for device shots:** only a real photo of the real device. If a device has no usable licensed photo, show a silhouette-free title card ("Nokia 3310, 2000") instead of a recreation.

**On-screen labels:** a small gold tag **FACT** or a dark-red tag **INTERPRETATION** appears at the bottom-left whenever the script marks it.

## Asset coverage summary
- **Real photographs [C]:** Nokia 1011, 1100, N95 (optional), Communicator 9000 (optional), 3310, 5800, N97 Mini, Lumia 800, N9, 808 PureView, original iPhone, Ollila, Elop — all located on Commons with licences (table below).
- **Stock [P]:** factory, office, tower, chess, crowd — all shots tie to a line.
- **Generated [G]:** 8 atmosphere/symbol shots, none showing a real brand, logo, device or person.
- **Gaps you need to fill by hand:** HTC Dream photo (lookup rate-limited), Steve Jobs at the 2007 keynote, a Nokia HQ photo, a Nokia factory photo (searches returned none), the *Ringtone* cover (don't use without clearance), and the Lumia launch event. Where a licensed image isn't found the storyboard falls back to a typographic card, never to a made-up device.
