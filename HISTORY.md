# Historical notes — what is documented, what is reconstruction

Setting: **Western Europe, ~30,000 years ago** (Late Ice Age; the Gravettian culture is established in Europe from roughly 32,000 years ago).
Sources consulted: [Gravettian (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravettian), [Discover Magazine on the Gravettians](https://www.discovermagazine.com/the-gravettian-culture-that-survived-an-ice-age-44651),
[University of Barcelona on Canyars leatherworking](https://web.ub.edu/en/web/actualitat/w/un-nou-estudi-revela-com-els-homo-sapiens-feien-roba-fa-39.000-anys), [Bone flute (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone_flute),
[Drake et al. 2015 on Paleolithic canids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5389137/).

| In the film | Status |
|---|---|
| People are anatomically modern *Homo sapiens*, hunter-gatherers living in small mobile groups | Documented |
| Very cold climate, mammoth-steppe landscape with mammoths, reindeer, horses, wolves | Documented (fauna and climate). The **forest is artistic licence**: much of Europe was open steppe-tundra, with wooded patches mostly in the south, so "enormous trees" are a storytelling choice |
| Sewn/tailored hide clothing | Plausible: sewing needles appear in the Gravettian and worked leather is known from ~39,600 years ago (Canyars, Spain). The **cut, colours and fur trim are reconstruction** |
| Flint blades, wooden spears with hafted stone points | Documented tool types in general; exact hafting and shapes are reconstruction. (Hand axes were deliberately left out: they belong to far older traditions) |
| Hearths and fire | Documented. **How fire was started** (spark + tinder shown) is reconstruction |
| Cave and rock-shelter use, hand stencils and animal paintings | Documented for Upper Palaeolithic Europe; the particular images shown are generic illustrations |
| Hide lean-to, meat-drying rack | Reconstruction. Structures existed (the evidence varies by region); these designs are illustrative |
| Bone flute | Documented: bone flutes from Swabian Jura caves date to at least ~35,000 years ago (some estimates ~40,000+). The **tune and timbre are invented** — no melody survives |
| Wolves | Documented as part of the fauna. Whether any wolves lived alongside people as "dogs" at this date is **debated** (e.g. the Goyet skull), so only wild wolves are shown |
| The locals' language (picture bubbles) | Invention: nothing is known about the languages spoken. Pictographs are used so no real language is faked |
| Skin tone | Genetic evidence on pre-Neolithic Europeans points to dark-to-intermediate skin; the locals are drawn brown accordingly |
| Berries and roots, grinding seeds, roasting meat | Plausible diet elements (hunted animals and gathered plants are both attested); specific recipes are not |
| "Odds of surviving the day" counter | A joke, not a statistic |
| The hero's beanie, cloak, fridge, phone, supermarket | Modern comedy intruding on the past, on purpose |

Nothing in the film uses farming, metal, medieval clothing or domestic animals.
