## Turkish-specific notes
- **Distances** are given in kilometres: "300.000 km'den fazla" (from 200,000+ miles ≈ 322,000 km) and the chip
  "~330.000 KM" (205,000 miles ≈ 330,000 km). Both are conversions, not sourced figures.
- **"Kızamıkçık"** = German measles / rubella (the sources name rubella).
- **Name respellings** for the voice: Lovell → "Lovel", Haise → "Hayz", Swigert → "Svaygırt", Mattingly → "Metingli",
  Houston → "Hyuston", Aquarius → "Akuaryus". On-screen captions keep the original spellings.
- **"Tamir bandı"** is used for "duct tape" (more natural than "koli bandı", which is packing tape).
- **Voice:** Piper `tr_TR-fahrettin-medium` (offline, male). I could not listen to it. Listen to the whole video
  before publishing; if it sounds flat, swap in `fettah` or `dfki` (same download, change the model path) or
  record a human voice-over and replace `public/apollo13-tr.mp3` (keep the length close to 2:34, or regenerate the
  timeline with your audio).
- **Native-speaker review:** have a Turkish speaker read the captions (`../captions/apollo13-tr.srt`) once.
- "Tayland mağara kurtarması · 18 gün" on the end card is from memory (reported durations are 17–18 days
  depending on how you count): verify when you script that story.
