# Chile 2010 — Part 2 (the rescue): fact sheet + outline

Gathered by web search on 2026-10-27. Anything marked **conflict** was left out of the plan on purpose.

## Confirmed by at least one source
- **Plan A**: drilling began **August 31, 2010**.
- **Plan B**: the Schramm T-130 drill (normally used for water wells) arrived **September 3**.
- **Plan C**: drilling started **September 22**.
- Plans A and C **stalled or veered off course**; Plan B won the "race".
- **Plan B broke through on October 9, 2010**, a little after 8 a.m., at about **2,041 feet**, after **33 days of drilling**.
  The drilling operator (Jeff Hart) was brought in from Afghanistan. A pilot hole was drilled first, then widened.
- **Phoenix capsule**: arrived at the mine **September 25**, painted **red, white and blue**; successfully tested **September 30**.
- **Rescue** began the night of **Tuesday October 12**; men came up **one at a time** in the capsule, about **15 minutes per trip**.
- **First miner out:** **Florencio Ávalos** (reported on October 13), chosen as one of the fittest.
- **Last miner out:** shift foreman **Luis Urzúa**, evening of **October 13**; the whole operation took **under 24 hours**.
- Urzúa to the president: *"We have done what the entire world was waiting for."* (CNN)
- **69 days** underground in total; reported as the longest anyone has been trapped underground and survived.

## Conflicts / unresolved (do not use a number)

- Capsule size: "13 feet tall" vs "1.9 metres long".

## Outline (≈ 45 lines, same structure as Apollo 13)
1. **Hook:** all 33 alive, but 2,000+ ft of rock above them.
2. **Lifeline:** small supply boreholes keep them fed and in contact (verify details first).
3. **The race:** Plan A (Aug 31), Plan B (Sept 3), Plan C (Sept 22); A and C go off course.
4. **Breakthrough:** Oct 9, Plan B reaches them after 33 days.
5. **Phoenix:** built, painted in the colours of the Chilean flag, tested Sept 30.
6. **Rescue night:** Oct 12–13, one man at a time, about 15 minutes each; Ávalos first.
7. **Last out:** Urzúa; the quote; 69 days; all 33.
8. **CTA:** next story.
