# Quecreek mine rescue (Pennsylvania, July 2002) — fact sheet

Gathered by web search on 2026-10-28. **S** = stated by at least one source found; conflicts are left out of the script.

## Confirmed (S)
- **July 24, 2002**: 18 miners went underground in two crews of nine (3 p.m. shift).
- **Around 9 p.m.** the crews broke into the abandoned **Saxman Mine** next door; it was flooded. One crew of nine escaped
  (out around 9:45 p.m.); a 911 call was made at 9:53 p.m.
- **Nine miners trapped about 240 feet underground** (one source says 190 ft; another speaks of 245 ft of rock to drill through).
- **Drilling began the next morning (July 25)**. A **6-inch hole** from the surface pumped air down, maintaining an air pocket
  and pressurizing the chamber. By noon (July 25) the water was within about 70 feet of the miners (single source: CBS timeline).
- A mine-safety official (Joe Sbaffoni, Bureau of Mine Safety, per Gov. Schweiker) studied maps to choose the drilling spot.
- Rescuers tapped on the air pipe; **three strong bangs** answered at 5:12 a.m. July 25 (Wikipedia); **nine taps** followed
  (11:40 a.m. per Wikipedia; "about two hours after the drill broke through" per KDKA). Gov. Mark Schweiker: nine taps in miners'
  code means "there are nine of us down here."
- **Just before 2 a.m. July 26** the drill bit broke. Crews began a second hole while removing the broken bit; by that afternoon the
  bit was out and drilling resumed.
- A larger "super drill" broke through on **July 27**; crews then heard that all nine were alive.
- A **24-inch hole**; a rescue **cage 22 inches wide** lifted the miners **one at a time**; the 4th miner was out early Sunday, **July 28**.
- All nine were rescued by July 28, **about 77 hours** after the flood (one source says 78).

## Conflicts / not used
- Flood volume: **50–60 million** (CNN fact sheet) vs **~75 million** (Atlas Obscura) vs **150 million** gallons (KDKA). Script says
  "more than fifty million" only if you accept the lowest sourced figure; the plan is to say "tens of millions of gallons".
- Depth 240 vs 190 ft: script says "about 240 feet".
- 77 vs 78 hours: script says "about 77".
- **Not verified:** that the mine map was wrong (widely reported, but I did not get a source in this pass), miners' farewell notes,
  tying themselves together, the exact dates of the shaft and cage arrival. Left out.
