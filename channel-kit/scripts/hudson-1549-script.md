# Hudson River landing (US Airways Flight 1549, Jan 15, 2009) — script for your voice-over

Target length: about 2:00 at a calm documentary pace (≈ 150 words per minute). 34 lines. **Line ids (h1, s1, …) must stay
the same** — the animation is keyed to them.

## How to send the audio (either way works)
1. **One file per line**, named by id: `h1.mp3`, `h2.mp3`, … (best: exact timing, no guessing), or
2. **One continuous file per language**, with **at least one second of silence between lines** and no extra words. I will split it
   on the silences and check that I get exactly 34 pieces; if not, I'll tell you which line to re-record.
Record in a quiet room, steady volume, no background music (I add the sound design). WAV or MP3 is fine.
