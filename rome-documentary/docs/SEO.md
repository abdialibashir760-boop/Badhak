## Description
How did a small settlement on the Tiber become an empire that ruled territories across Europe, Africa and Asia?

In four minutes, this animated documentary follows Rome from its legendary founding (753 BCE — tradition, not archaeology) through the Roman Republic, the Punic Wars against Carthage and Hannibal's crossing of the Alps, Julius Caesar and the Rubicon (49 BCE), the end of the Republic, and Augustus — Rome's first emperor (27 BCE) — to the empire's greatest extent under Trajan in 117 CE.

Every map is built programmatically on an ancient-parchment style chart, with territory, borders, roads, trade routes and frontiers animated by date. The narration separates what historians can document from what Romans only said about themselves.
