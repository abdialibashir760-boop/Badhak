# 06 · Thumbnails, Titles, SEO

## Three thumbnail concepts

**1 — “The Last Light.”** A tiny submersible light cone in endless black water. Text: **NO SUNLIGHT**. Sub-line: *10,935 m*. Colours: black, navy, one warm light. Why it works: scale and isolation with one clear idea.

**2 — “Pressure.”** Real steel pressure sphere in dark water, tiny viewport glowing. Text: **1,000× PRESSURE**. Why it works: a single number, a real object, no monsters.

**3 — “Who lives here?”** A pale, real-looking amphipod in macro against black. Text: **WHAT LIVES HERE?** Why it works: the central question, a real animal.

> Thumbnail rules: no invented monsters, no misleading text, no fake 'giant' animals. Label as illustration if generated.
