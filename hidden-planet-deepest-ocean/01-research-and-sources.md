## What the script avoids on purpose

- **No fish at the bottom.** The famous 1960 *Trieste* "flatfish" sighting is doubted; some suggest a sea cucumber. The script says so.
- **No giant creatures or monsters.** The script names no animal that is not documented.
- **No animal is placed deeper than its record.** Anglerfish, hatchetfish and jellies are explicitly shown as living in shallower zones (the on-screen caption states the depth).
- **No vent animals at the trench floor.** Vents are described as a separate environment.
- **No claim that "nothing lives" or "everything lives" at depth.** Every claim is dated and attributed.

## Disagreements between sources and how they were handled

| Topic | Sources | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Depth of Challenger Deep | N1: 10,935 ± 6 m; other surveys 10,902–10,929 m; one search result gave 10,984 m (older) | Use 10,935 ± 6 and show the spread in a diagram |
| Pressure | 1,086 bar (W2), 1,089 atm, 111 MPa, "16,000 psi" (various) | Say "more than a thousand atmospheres, about 16,000 pounds per square inch" |
| Fish depth limit | P3: ≈ 8,200 m (prediction); some sources 8,000–8,500 m; 8,370 m catch and 8,336 m film report | Say "a hypothesis supported by data, not an absolute law"; "filmed deeper than 8,000 m"; no fish reported in the deepest quarter |
| Deepest fish caught | 7,966 m (corroborated); 8,370 m (possibly a non-closing net, uncertain) | Use 7,966 m as the "documented capture" |
| *H. gigas* diet | P1: enzymes found, gut contents not directly confirmed | Say "carry enzymes that can digest cellulose" and "may be food" |
