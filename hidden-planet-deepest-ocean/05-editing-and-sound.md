# 05 · Editing Timeline, Camera Movement and Sound Design

**Total:** 9:15 · **Shots:** 139 · **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4

## Rules

- **Composition changes every 3–6 s on average** (this plan: 4.0 s). Hold 6–8 s on organisms and key reveals.
- **Motion:** every still gets slow motion (push-in / pull-back / pan 100% → 105–110%). Depth transitions: dissolve down, with particles in parallax. No whip pans.
- **Colour:** deep navy, black, subtle cyan; natural bioluminescent blue-green only. Grade stock and archive to match (lower saturation, lift nothing).
- **Captions:** every real animal gets a lower-third: common name, scientific name, and *documented depth range*. Diagrams use plain labels.
- **Sound:** subtle underwater ambience, low-frequency tones, restrained music, and silence at key moments (after 'So what could possibly live here?', at the pressure reveal, at the end). Never a horror stinger.
- **Loudness:** voice −16 LUFS, mix −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP. Music ducks 18 dB under voice.
- **On-screen note (first 20 s and end card):** which visuals are real footage, stock, generated, or diagrams.
